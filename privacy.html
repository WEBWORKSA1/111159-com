Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Who we are

111159.com ("we", "us") is an independent website. You can reach us through our contact form.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through forms: name, email, company, phone or WhatsApp, and request details for sourcing, contests, donations, careers or advertising.
  • Usage data (if you accept analytics cookies): pages viewed, device, approximate location, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Local storage on your device: theme preference, cookie choice, form drafts, deal votes and your referral code. This stays in your browser.

How we use it

  • To answer inquiries and deliver the newsletters and alerts you requested.
  • To share sourcing and brand requests with vetted partners when you consent, so they can prepare quotes.
  • To run contests and contact winners.
  • To improve the site and measure performance.

Form processing

Forms are delivered by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-to-email service. Submissions pass through their servers to reach us.

Advertising & Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Embedded content

YouTube videos load via youtube-nocookie.com only after you click play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California, PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25 in Canada, DPDP Act in India), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing a sourcing request with partners happens only with your consent.

Retention & security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave, or as the law requires. The site is served over HTTPS.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and contests are for adults only.

Changes

We'll post any updates on this page with a new date.

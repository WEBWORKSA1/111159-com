Official Rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  2. Eligibility: open to individuals aged 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). Employees of 111159 and prize sponsors, and their immediate families, are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: as shown in the timeline above. Late or incomplete entries are void. One form entry per person; bonus entries as described.
  4. Selection: the grand prize and runner-up prizes are judged on creativity (50%), story (30%) and cultural insight (20%). Instant-win codes are drawn at random. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries.
  5. Prizes: as announced on this page before the entry period. They are not transferable, and there is no cash alternative unless required by law. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.
  6. Skill-testing question: Canadian winners must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question.
  7. Québec residents: any dispute about running this contest may be submitted to the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux. Where required, the contest will be registered with the Régie before launch.
  8. Notification: winners are contacted by email and must respond within 7 days, or an alternate winner is selected.
  9. Content licence: by entering, you grant 111159 a non-exclusive licence to publish your entry (first name and country) in connection with the contest.
  10. Privacy: entry data is used only to run the contest and, if you opt in, for newsletters. See the Privacy Policy.

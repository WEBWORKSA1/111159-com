The name

Why "111159"?

Read aloud the way Chinese people read phone numbers, 1-1-1-1-5-9 is yāo-yāo-yāo-yāo-wǔ-jiǔ. That sounds like 要要要要，我久: "want, want, want, want… me, forever."

  • 1111: Singles' Day, the four "bare sticks" of 11 November
  • 1 (yāo): sounds like 要, "want"
  • 5 (wǔ): sounds like 我, "I / me"
  • 9 (jiǔ): sounds like 久, "long-lasting"

About us

Want it all, for good.

111159 is an independent guide to the world's biggest shopping festival, and to the numbers, culture and supply chains behind it.

Our mission

Make 11.11 simple, safe and rewarding for everyone: shoppers hunting real deals, small businesses importing their first container, and brands going global.

What we do

  • Track and verify Singles' Day sales across platforms and regions
  • Build free tools: countdown, coupon stacker, landed-cost and currency calculators
  • Connect importers with vetted sourcing partners
  • Explain Chinese number culture: why 8 is lucky and what 111159 means

Editorial standards

  • Deals are verified by a human before they're featured.
  • Sponsored placements are always labelled.
  • Advertisers never influence our ratings, votes or guides.
  • Statistics link to their sources, and we correct errors quickly. Report an issue.

How we make money

Display ads (including Google AdSense), affiliate commissions, sponsorships, fees from sourcing partners for qualified requests, and reader support. Full disclosure →

FAQ

Sourcing questions

Is requesting quotes really free?
Yes. There is no charge and no obligation to request quotes. If you go ahead, sourcing partners quote their service fees transparently before any work starts.
What's the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
It depends on the product. Many factories accept 100–500 units for custom items, and in-stock items can ship from a single unit. Tell us your quantity and we'll find suppliers that fit.
How fast will I hear back?
Most requests get a first response within 24 hours on business days. A full quote comparison usually takes 3–7 days, depending on how complex the product is.
How do I avoid scams and bad quality?
Use verified suppliers, always order samples, pay through protected methods, and book a pre-shipment inspection before paying the balance. See our scam checklist.
How are duties and taxes calculated?
Import duty usually applies to the customs value (goods plus freight in many countries), and then VAT/GST is added. Use our landed-cost calculator, and confirm rates with your customs broker.

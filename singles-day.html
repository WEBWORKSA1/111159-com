Origin: four bare sticks

The date 11/11 is written with four "1"s, which look like four bare sticks (光棍 guānggùn), Chinese slang for single people. Students at Nanjing University are credited with celebrating an "anti-Valentine's" Bachelors' Day around 1993. In 2009, Alibaba's Taobao Mall (now Tmall) ran an 11.11 promotion with just 27 merchants. Sales reached about ¥50 million, and a retail phenomenon was born.

How the modern festival works

  • Pre-sale and deposits (预售 / 定金): from mid-to-late October you can lock in a price with a small deposit and pay the balance in the first 11.11 wave.
  • Multiple waves: most platforms now run several sale windows from late October through 11 November, and some extend past it.
  • Threshold coupons (满减): "spend ¥300, save ¥50" stacks with store discounts. Use our coupon stacker.
  • Live-stream shopping: hosts and influencers sell live with exclusive codes. It's a huge share of sales.
  • Price guarantees: many platforms promise to refund the difference if the price drops within the period. Check each platform's rules.
  • Midnight drops: flash deals go live at 00:00 Beijing time (UTC+8), which is 11:00 am the day before in New York.

Singles' Day outside China

Cross-border marketplaces and many Western, Southeast Asian and Middle Eastern retailers now run 11.11 sales, often as a warm-up to Black Friday. For shoppers outside China, the key things are import duties, shipping times (order early for holiday gifts) and avoiding counterfeits.

# 111159.com: Research Brief & Website Idea

## 1. What "111159" means in Chinese culture, economy and daily life

There is **no single fixed meaning** of "111159" in Chinese. Its value comes from three layers of symbolism that Chinese speakers read almost automatically.

| Layer | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **1111** | 光棍节 *Guānggùn Jié*, "bare-branches / Singles' Day", 11 November | The four "1"s look like four bare sticks, so 11/11 became a day for single people. It started at Nanjing University in 1993 and became the **world's largest shopping festival** after Alibaba adopted it in 2009. |
| **1 = yāo (幺)** | In phone numbers, 1 is spoken *yāo*, which sounds like **要 yào**, "want" | 1111 reads as "want-want-want-want". |
| **5 = wǔ** | Sounds like **我 wǒ** ("I / me"), as in 520 = 我爱你, "I love you" | Personal. |
| **9 = jiǔ** | Sounds like **久 jiǔ** ("long-lasting, forever") | Longevity; a lucky number, and a favourite for weddings and brands. |
| **59** | 我久 / 我就, "me, forever" or "I'm in" | |

**Composite phone-style reading:** *yāo-yāo-yāo-yāo-wǔ-jiǔ*, which sounds like 要要要要，我久, loosely **"Want it all, for good."**

**Composite visual reading:** **11·11 + 59**, the Singles'-Day countdown feel of "11:59, one minute to midnight", when the pre-sale opens.

**Economic layer:** 11.11 is the largest retail event on earth.

- 2009: Tmall's first edition had **27 merchants** and about **¥50 million** in sales.
- 2021: Alibaba reported **¥540.3 billion (~US$84.5 billion)** over 11 days. It then stopped publishing GMV.
- 2024: Syntun's all-platform estimate was **¥1.44 trillion (~US$203 billion), up 26.6%**.
- 2025: **¥1.695 trillion (~US$238 billion), up 14.2%**.
- Peak processing reached **583,000 orders per second** (2020).
- 2024 GMV was roughly **10× US online Black Friday spend**.

**People / language layer:** Number slang is everyday Chinese internet language: 520 (I love you), 1314 (一生一世, forever), 88 (bye), 666 (awesome), 8 (发, wealth), 4 (死, avoided). Chinese users strongly favour **numeric domains** because they are easier to type and remember than pinyin. Examples are 163.com, 360.cn, 58.com, 4399.com and 12306.cn.

**Finance note:** China's securities codes are six-digit numbers. On the Shanghai exchange the 11xxxx range is used for bond instruments. We found **no** notable listing or brand tied to "111159".

## 2. Trademark check (why we avoid certain words)

- Alibaba filed **"DOUBLE 11" / "双11" / "双十一"** marks from November 2011. They were registered in 2012–2013 in classes 35, 38 and 41.
- Those marks have since faced invalidation, non-use cancellation and genericness challenges, including a suit filed by JD.com.
- **Decision:** the brand is **"111159"**. We use "Singles' Day" and "11.11" only as descriptive, nominative terms, never "Double 11 / 双11" as branding. There are no platform logos, and every page carries a trademark disclaimer.

## 3. Website idea (chosen)

**111159.com: the global 11.11 Deals, Countdown & China-Sourcing Hub.** Tagline: *"Want it all, for good."*

Why this idea wins on traffic × revenue:

1. **Traffic.** "singles day", "11.11 sale", "aliexpress 11.11 coupon" and similar terms bring a large seasonal search spike every October and November. The site also carries year-round evergreen demand: Chinese number meanings, how to buy from China, and landed-cost calculators.
2. **Revenue stack.**
   - AdSense: seasonal RPM peaks in Q4.
   - Affiliate: AliExpress, Temu, Amazon, Banggood and similar programmes.
   - **B2B sourcing leads**: RFQs sold to vetted sourcing agents. This is the highest-value stream; one qualified import lead is worth far more than a pageview.
   - Sponsorships (countdown "presented by", newsletter, contests).
   - YouTube.
   - Donations.
3. **Moat.** An exact-match numeric .com, plus interactive tools (countdown, number decoder, coupon-stacking calculator, landed-cost calculator) that keep earning links.

## 4. Benchmark audit (28 sites visited)

The sites audited were: Slickdeals, DealNews, HotUKDeals, mydealz, OzBargain, Dealmoon (11.11 hub), Brad's Deals, 9to5Toys, Blacker Friday, CouponFollow, Rakuten, SMZDM (什么值得买), Alizila, Aliholic, timeanddate, China Highlights, TravelChinaGuide, Infini Digital, Alibaba RFQ, Made-in-China Easy Sourcing, Global Sources, LeelineSourcing, Jingsourcing, Supplyia, Gleam, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi and Kickstarter.

Features adopted from them:

- **Timing and deal display:** a timezone-aware countdown with a pre-sale → peak → extended timeline; deal cards with was/now price, %-off and badges; filters by platform and category; an expired-deal toggle.
- **Community:** hot-voting; a 4-field "submit a deal" form.
- **Sourcing RFQ:** a 4-step form with starred fields, hints, a progress bar and a trust strip; the CTA reads "Get free quotes in 24h".
- **Alerts:** keyword deal alerts.
- **Contests:** a Gleam-style form with bonus entries and official "no purchase necessary" rules.
- **Donations:** preset tip amounts in the style of Buy Me a Coffee and Ko-fi.
- **Advertising:** a rate card.
- **Content:** FAQ and Article schema, and affiliate disclosure on every page.
- **Tools and interface:** a timeanddate-style embeddable countdown, dark mode, and fixed-size ad slots so the layout doesn't jump.
